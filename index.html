The creek trails of the South Bay

Paths follow the creeks and old rail lines of San José from the mountains toward the Bay. Most of the mileage is open. The gaps are short, expensive and stubborn: a trestle, a culvert, a back yard, a BART station. Pick a trail to see what's built, what's missing, what it has cost, and every document and news story behind it.

    About these pages

    Each trail page brings together public records: city council memos and GIS data, Valley Water and County Parks documents, environmental filings, and local journalism. Every event, cost and figure links to its source, and each page lists what we couldn't verify.

    An independent project, not affiliated with the City of San José, Santa Clara County, Valley Water, VTA or any of the other agencies involved.

    • Gap-cost figures on the trail pages are our extrapolations unless marked as official estimates.
    • GIS status can lag what's on the ground. Each trail page notes where we know it does.
